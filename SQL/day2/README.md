#  Day 2 – SQL Aggregate & Group By Queries

##  Overview

Today’s practice focused on **Aggregate Functions**, **GROUP BY**, and **HAVING clauses** in SQL.
These concepts are used to summarize data and perform analysis on grouped records.

## Topics Covered

* Aggregate Functions:

  * `SUM()`
  * `AVG()`
  * `MIN()`
  * `MAX()`
  * `COUNT()`
* `GROUP BY` clause
* `HAVING` clause (filtering grouped data)

##  Queries Implemented

###  Aggregate Queries

* Total salary of all employees
* Average salary of employees
* Minimum salary
* Count of employees per department
* Average salary per department

###  Group By Queries

* Total salary for each department
* Average age per department
* Employees hired per year
* Highest salary per department
* Department with highest average salary

### Having Queries

* Departments with more than 2 employees
* Departments with average salary > 55000
* Years with more than 1 employee hired
* Departments with total salary < 100000
* Departments with max salary > 75000
